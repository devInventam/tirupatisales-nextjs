What Is an Intelligent LED Driver?

An LED driver is a power supply device specifically designed for LED lighting systems. It converts AC power into the required DC voltage and current needed by LEDs.

Intelligent LED drivers include advanced features such as:

  • Programmable output current
  • DALI and 0–10V dimming support
  • Thermal protection
  • Power factor correction
  • Smart lighting compatibility
  • Wireless control readiness

How to Choose the Right LED Driver

1. Select Output Type

Choose between Constant Current (CC) and Constant Voltage (CV) drivers based on your LED fixture requirements.

2. Match Wattage Properly

Always keep 10–20% power headroom for longer driver lifespan and stable performance.

3. Verify Dimming Compatibility

Confirm whether the project requires DALI, 0–10V, TRIAC, or PWM dimming support.

4. Check Environmental Ratings

Outdoor and industrial projects require IP65 or IP67 rated LED drivers.

5. Buy From Authorized Suppliers

Always purchase genuine LED drivers from authorized distributors to ensure warranty and technical support.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

Frequently Asked Questions

What is an LED driver?

An LED driver is a regulated power supply that provides the correct voltage and current for LED fixtures.

What is the difference between Constant Current and Constant Voltage drivers?

Constant Current drivers provide fixed current output, while Constant Voltage drivers provide fixed voltage output.

How long do LED drivers last?

Premium LED drivers can last between 50,000 and 100,000 operational hours.

What is DALI dimming?

DALI is a digital lighting control protocol used for intelligent lighting systems and centralized control.

Why should I buy from authorized suppliers?
